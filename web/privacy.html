Privacy Policy

What Should I Do? (WSID?) · Effective date: November 16, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how What Should I Do? (also shown as “WSID?”, the “App”, “we”, “us”) collects, uses, stores and protects your information when you use our mobile, desktop and web applications. By using the App, you agree to the practices described here.

1. Information We Collect

Account information

  • Email address and password you provide when you create an account. Passwords are hashed by our authentication provider and are never visible to us.
  • Display name you choose for your profile.

Content you create

  • Tasks, including title, notes, optional links, estimated duration, priority, status (pending, in progress, completed) and related timestamps.
  • Categories you create to organise your tasks.

Information stored only on your device

  • App preferences such as theme, language and whether reminders are enabled.
  • Scheduled local notifications.

We do not collect your precise location, contacts, photos, advertising identifiers or payment information, and the App contains no third-party advertising or analytics SDKs.

2. How We Use Your Information

  • To create and secure your account and sign you in.
  • To store, sync and display your tasks and categories across your devices.
  • To suggest a task for you to work on, based only on your own task list.
  • To show optional reminders about your active task.
  • To maintain, troubleshoot and improve the reliability of the App.

We do not sell your personal information, and we do not use it for advertising or profiling.

3. Authentication and Cloud Storage (Supabase)

The App uses Supabase as its backend provider for user authentication and cloud database storage. Your account details, tasks and categories are stored in a Supabase-hosted PostgreSQL database. Access is protected by encrypted connections (HTTPS/TLS) and row-level security rules, so each user can only read and modify their own data.

Supabase processes this data on our behalf as a data processor. You can review its practices in the Supabase Privacy Policy. Depending on where Supabase infrastructure is located, your data may be processed outside your country of residence, with appropriate safeguards in place.

4. Local Push Notifications

The App may schedule local notifications on your device to remind you about your active task. These notifications are generated and delivered entirely on your device; their content is not sent to us or to any third-party push service. You can turn reminders off at any time in the App’s Profile → Preferences screen or in your device’s system settings.

5. Links to External Websites

Tasks can contain links you add yourself. Opening a link takes you to a third-party website or app that is governed by its own privacy policy. We are not responsible for the content or practices of those sites.

6. Data Retention

We keep your account and content for as long as your account is active. When you delete your account, your profile, tasks and categories are permanently removed from our database. Residual copies in encrypted backups maintained by our provider are overwritten in the normal backup cycle.

7. Your Rights and Choices

  • Access and correction: view and edit your display name, tasks and categories directly in the App.
  • Deletion: delete your account and all associated data in the App via Profile → Delete Account.
  • Notifications: enable or disable reminders at any time.
  • Depending on your location (for example, under the GDPR or CCPA), you may have additional rights such as data portability or the right to object to processing. Contact us to exercise them.

8. Security

We use industry-standard measures, including encrypted transport, hashed passwords and per-user access controls, to protect your information. No method of transmission or storage is 100% secure, so we cannot guarantee absolute security.

9. Children’s Privacy

The App is not directed at children under 13 (or the minimum age required in your country). We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided us with personal data, please contact us so we can delete it.

10. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. When we do, we will revise the effective date above and, where appropriate, notify you in the App. Continued use of the App after changes take effect means you accept the updated policy.

11. Contact Us

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy or your data, contact us at support@your-domain.com.
